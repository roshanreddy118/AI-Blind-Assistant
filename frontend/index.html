About

AI Blind Assistant helps visually impaired users navigate the world using real-time AI vision. Point your camera at anything — the app describes scenes, reads text/signs, detects obstacles, and answers questions, all spoken aloud in 14+ languages.

👁️‍🗨️ Welcome to AI Blind Assistant

Your AI-powered eyes. This app helps visually impaired users navigate the world in real time.

  • 🔍
    Scene Description — Point your camera, get instant spoken narration
  • 📖
    Text Reading — Reads signs, labels, menus & documents aloud
  • ⚠️
    Obstacle Detection — Warns you about nearby hazards
  • ❓
    Ask AI — Ask any question about what's in front of you
  • 🌐
    14+ Languages — Works in English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu & more

Tap "Tap to Start" to begin. You can also use voice commands starting with "Assistant" — e.g. "Assistant start", "Assistant stop", "Assistant English", "Assistant Telugu".
